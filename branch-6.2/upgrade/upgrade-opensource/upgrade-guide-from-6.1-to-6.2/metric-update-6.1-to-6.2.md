# ScyllaDB Metric Update - ScyllaDB 6.1 to 6.2

ScyllaDB 6.2 Dashboards are available as part of the latest [Scylla Monitoring Stack](https://monitoring.docs.scylladb.com).

## New Metrics

The following metrics are new in ScyllaDB 6.2:

| Metric                             | Description                                            |
|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| scylla_alternator_batch_item_count | The total number of items processed across all batches |
